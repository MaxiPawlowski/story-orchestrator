## T0 blocker: commit evidence after rollback

Session T0-3 (`test/sessions/T0/T0-3-1`, story `adolion-adventurer`): accept the Wendhope job (story moves to The Road North), edit that line into a refusal (correct rollback to The Guild Hall), then re-accept three times (mes 9 "Fine, we'll do it", mes 11, mes 14 "we take the Wendhope job"). The story never left the Guild Hall (flag `turns.jsonl:20`, SUMMARY HIGH).

- **Not a rollback-state bug.** After the edit rollback the blackboard was clean (`runtime-*.json` `engineHistory`: no `path`, only `party_name` latched; the gate `path=="wendhope" && party_name!=""` therefore waited on `path` alone). No held-commitment record, `answered` set or latch survives a rollback: `held` is only journaled, `answered` is per read.
- **Root cause: the commit guard judged the line the reader happened to quote** (`src/extraction/commitGuard.ts:44` before the fix: `matcher.test(delta.evidence)`). Every read after the rollback extracted `path=wendhope` (`journal.jsonl` audits 316, 332, 396, 428, 440, 480, 499, 550) but cited the NPC proof of it, Tobias handing over the notice ("Your posting.", `journal.jsonl:470`), which has no commitment verb, so all were held ("commitment reading(s) held" x11, journal.jsonl:222-601). The first acceptance passed only because that read quoted the player ("This one. We take Wendhope.", audit 74).
- **Second defect in the same check (agency):** the guard never asked WHO wrote the line. An NPC "So you've agreed then" or narration "Max nods and accepts" matched the pattern (`agree(?:s|d)?`, `accept(?:s|ed)?`) and would have committed the player.
- **Fix** (`src/extraction/commitGuard.ts:39-52`, call site `src/runtime/runtimeManager.ts:435`): a commit-evidence delta is accepted only when a line the PLAYER wrote (`isUser`) inside the read's window matches the quality's `commit_evidence`. The quote no longer decides it, and NPC/narrator lines never count. The manager reads the window lazily from the host chat (`chatWindow(window.from, window.to)`), only when a batch carries a guarded quality, so an edited line is judged as it stands now. Judge-typed and stall deltas take the same rule. Prompt unchanged (option (a) not needed: the guard no longer depends on the cited line, and changing the shared-read prompt would move its contract hash). Studio help text updated.
- **Known limit closed (follow-up, same day):** the player-line match is negation- and value-aware (`src/extraction/commitGuard.ts`). A match counts only when (1) no negator (`not`, `never`, `no`, `won't`/`don't`/`can't`/any `n't`, `cannot`, `refuse`, `decline`; curly apostrophes normalised) stands before the verb in the same clause (clauses break on `. ! ? , ; : ( ) —` and `but`/`though`/`although`), and (2) its sentence is about the claimed value: one of the value's words (`wendhope`) or the `extractor_trigger` of a transition whose gate reads the quality. A verb that points back (`we'll do it`, `count us in`, `take it`) needs the window, any speaker, to be about the value instead. A quality with neither value words nor a transition intent (a bool with no trigger) keeps the verb-only rule; negation still applies. Refusals that no longer commit: "We won't take it", "We will not accept the Wendhope job", "No, we don't agree", "I'd never take that job", "I refuse to sign anything for Wendhope"; unrelated uses: "I'll take a seat", "We'll go to the bar first". Still committing: "Fine, we'll do it", "We take Wendhope", "No, not that one. We take Wendhope", "I won't lie, it scares me, but we accept the posting". Remaining limit: a double negative ("I don't see why we wouldn't take it") reads as a refusal (fails safe, holds), and a pointing-back acceptance in a window that mentions the posting for another reason still commits. Tests: `src/extraction/commitNegation.test.ts` (12 of 19 red before, all green after).
- **Tests (red on the old guard, green on the fix):** `src/runtime/commitAfterRollback.review.test.ts` (accept, edit to refusal, rollback, re-accept with the reader quoting Tobias: the story reaches The Road North; NPC "you've agreed then" alone holds; narration "Max nods and accepts" holds) and `src/extraction/commitGuard.test.ts` (NPC-quoted read over a player commitment accepts; NPC and narrator never commit). 6 of 12 cases failed before the fix.
- **Related, T0-1 (`test/sessions/T0/T0-1-1/journal.jsonl:308`): an accepted commitment was thrown away by a read that never contradicted it.** The `scene:time_skip` read (msgs 6-13) accepted `path=wendhope` from the player's own "We'll take the Wendhope job." and queued it; the `reconcile:path,...` read (msgs 0-13) was silent on `path`, and at boundary 9 the apply queue discarded the whole older entry, `path` included, as "superseded: a newer read covered the same turns". Root cause: `src/engine/applyQueue.ts:58` (before the fix) superseded whole entries by turn range, whatever keys the newer read wrote. A held or rejected delta never reaches the queue, so a read that held or rejected the commitment had the same effect as one that omitted it. Fix: supersession is per key; an older write is dropped only for a key a newer covering read wrote again, the rest of the entry applies (tension levels travel with the tension delta). The 17 "evidence only in the player's line" rejections in T0-1 are all `evidence_from: "world"` qualities (`first_camp` 7, `adv_looking_for_hands` 6, `location` 4), by design; none was on `path`, and T0-1 held no commitment reading. Tests: `src/engine/engine.test.ts` ("a newer covering read that is silent on a key does not supersede it", tension split), `src/runtime/commitAfterRollback.review.test.ts` ("a later read over the same turns that rejects or omits the commitment does not supersede it"); `engine.test.ts`'s old "discards covered stale entries" and `journalContract.review.test.ts`'s origin case asserted the defect (a silent newer read discarding a key) and were rewritten so the newer read writes the key again.
- **Live re-check:** a fresh T0-3 run (see the lead's report for the exact driver steps); pass = after the edit rollback, the first re-acceptance turn reaches The Road North and the journal shows no "commitment reading(s) held" for it.
