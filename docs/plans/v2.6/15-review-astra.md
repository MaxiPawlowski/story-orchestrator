**v2.6 is not ready to start the planned acceptance campaign.** The principal blockers are feature reachability, incomplete measurement tooling, and evidence collection that can silently miss the behavior being judged.

Top 10:

1. **AS-11:** The campaign pin excludes the new chapters, drives and motives.
2. **AS-6:** Resealing can resume in another chat after ownership lapses.
3. **AS-2:** Concurrent bridge opens bypass both concurrency and owner limits.
4. **AS-3:** The ×2 predicate accepts an explicitly forbidden flaky series.
5. **AS-21:** Wizard measurement commands do not exercise the native harness bridge.
6. **AS-22:** Session capture failures do not reliably invalidate a run.
7. **AS-23:** Wizard-created chats and necessary rollback evidence are not exported.
8. **AS-13:** Required reasoning, intent and saga measurement tools remain unbuilt.
9. **AS-1:** Intent admission accepts player-invented intentions.
10. **AS-25:** Several default-off features have no effective charter coverage.

Read-only review; no files modified or live/model calls made. I ran isolated in-memory reproductions for AS-1, AS-2, AS-3, AS-6 and AS-7. Repository gate results were inspected, not rerun. Master advanced during review; the last observed HEAD was `177d9630`.
