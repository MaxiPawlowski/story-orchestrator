# v2.4 seeds — recorded, not built

Plan 11 §Findings register and v2.4 seeds asks for anything bounced to be listed **with its reason**.
This is that list, collected across v2.3. Nothing here was built or half-built: each row says why it
stopped and what would close it. A seed is not a promise — the judge rows in particular exist because
their own plan's rule is that a family below its declared floor is recorded as **not built**.

Where a seed has a measurement, the measurement is named and its artifact given, so a v2.4 agent
argues with the number rather than from it.

## E. The save-evidence asymmetry, stated so it is not "unified" by accident

Written up on 2026-09-22 while fixing the queue's guard (plan 05 §A decision that was never
written). Two consumers ask the same-looking question and must keep different answers, because their
failure modes are not each other's:

| consumer | question | refuses on | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `EffectsApplier.withLedger` | may I touch the **host** with a write I cannot record? | `hasUnsavedChanges` — the STICKY reading, including a read-back that could not speak | a checkpoint's world info switching on an unrecorded write is the failure the write-ahead row exists to prevent, and "could not verify" is exactly when the row might be memory-only |
| `memoryQueue.commitDecision` | may I tell the **author** their decision is settled? | `saveWasLost` — evidence only (no request, non-2xx, or a read-back showing an older boundary) | refusing on an unverifiable read-back would block the only actor who can make the decision, for a write that in fact landed |

Neither is a bug. Unifying them either lets an unrecorded host write through or blocks the author on
"could not confirm". If a future plan wants one predicate, it has to pick which failure moves.
