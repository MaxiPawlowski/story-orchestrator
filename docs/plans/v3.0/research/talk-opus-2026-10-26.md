# Talk control and pacing: independent review (Opus, 2026-10-26)

Read-only review. Nothing was built or run: every finding comes from reading the code at `59074a95`, and the ones marked *suspected* need a test before anyone acts on them. This was written without reading `talk-pacing-review-2026-10-26.md`, any `19-*` plan, or the `v3.0-talk-pacing` branch.

Owner's ask from the playtest: after the narrator sets the scene, a party member should speak straight away, for example by turning to the player and asking something. More pacing controls are wanted too.

## TL;DR

- **The opening the owner wants cannot be authored reliably today.** Each of the four routes breaks somewhere (section 1.2). The worst problem is that an `llm` npc_reply ignores its `instruction` everywhere (`effectSteps.ts:66-70`), even though the guide says it follows it (`story-guide.md:326`).
- Speaker direction only runs on a player send. Nothing reacts to the chat opening, or to a narrator line that hands the moment to a party member.
- No deterministic rule says "a character just asked the player a question, so stop the chain". A heuristic for it already exists, but only opener deferral uses it, and there it works against the goal: it defers the party hook.
- The default agency clause `NO_CLOSING_QUESTION_CLAUSE` is sent to every speaker and pushes a small model away from ending on a question.
- Fix order: deliver the instruction as a one-shot cue → add an `opening` sequence → add a question-yield rule → scope the question clause by speaker → add metrics, then spotlight rotation and pacing profiles.

## 1. Critical review

### 1.1 How a turn runs today (map)

- **Boundaries** come only from rendered character replies. `first_message` and `extension` posts are not turns (`turnBridge.ts:16-20`). After each rendered reply `enqueueBoundary` fires the `afterSpeak` npc_replies (`turnBridge.ts:204-208`, `runtimeManager.ts:334-344`).
- **Speaker direction** (`talkControl.ts`) acts only on a loud, non-forced `normal` generation in a group, while the active checkpoint has a `talk_control` (`talkControl.ts:213-218`; `runtimeManager.ts:381-384` returns null without one).
  - The first voice is picked in `computeDecision` (`:421-445`). Order: scripted sequence → judge → a single name mention in the last line → LLM director → weighted rules.
  - The chain continues in `advanceChain`/`decideChainSpeaker` (`:278-359`). Order: unanswered addressees of the player's line → judge/director with "hand back" offered → stop.
  - The defaults are on with `max` 3 (`settingsModel.ts:249`), but they apply only where a `talk_control` is declared.
- **npc_replies** (`effectsApplier.ts:471-516`) post through `/sendas` (scripted) or a bare `/trigger` (llm) (`effectSteps.ts:66-75`). Fire counts are keyed `checkpoint:trigger:member:index` and persist. `afterSpeak` waits at least 8 messages between fires (`npcReplyRewind.ts:19,33`).
- **Opening:**
  - Every card's `first_mes` greets at once, before `CHAT_CHANGED`.
  - The story's own opener is one scripted `onEnter` reply with `new_chat_only` (`validate/checkpoints.ts:129-130`). It fires first in `applyCheckpointNow` once requirements are ready and identity has settled (`effectsApplier.ts:186-189,212`).
  - Other `onEnter` replies fire at the end of activation (`:262`, `fireOnEnter :437-449`). They are deferred if the last chat line "awaits the player" (`openerDeferral.ts:44-47`).

### 1.2 Can "after the opening narration, a party member addresses the player with a question" be authored and fire reliably at chat start?

**No.** Each route breaks:

| Route | What breaks | Where |
|---|---|---|
| A. Give the party member's card a `first_mes` | It fires, but as fixed card text: no story context, card-global (every chat with that card greets this way), not story-owned, all greetings post at once. The guide tells authors not to do this (`story-guide.md` "The opening scene"). | ST host |
| B. Two `new_chat_only` scripted replies (narrator, then party) | **The second one never posts.** The empty-chat check runs per reply inside the loop, so after the first `/sendas` the chat has one message and the second is skipped silently. | `effectsApplier.ts:488` |
| C. A `new_chat_only` narrator opener plus a plain `onEnter` party reply with `kind: "llm"` | (1) **The `instruction` is dropped**: `fireReply` runs `/trigger await=true <pos>` with no injection, so the member generates blind and may narrate scenery instead of asking anything (`effectSteps.ts:66-70`; no other reader of `reply.instruction` exists in `src/runtime`). (2) **Deferral works backwards here**: `fireOnEnter` calls `holdOpener`, and if the opener's last sentence ends in `?`, or either of its last two quoted lines holds "you", the party line waits until *the player* has answered (`openerDeferral.ts:24-30,44-47`). That deferral was built for mid-story transitions and doesn't know about the opening. (3) *Suspected ordering race:* `openNewChat` waits for identity to settle (`effectsApplier.ts:187`) but `fireOnEnter` waits only for `ready` (`:260-262`). With a player identity step pending, the party line can post first into the empty chat; when identity settles the narrator opener then sees a non-empty chat and is skipped (`:488`). `playerSetupActivation.test.ts` covers only the lone `new_chat_only` opener (line 93), so the race is untested. | as cited |
| D. `afterSpeak` with `after_member: Narrator` | Neither a `first_mes` greeting nor our own opener counts. Greetings are not turns (`turnBridge.ts:20`), and replies we posted ourselves are excluded by `speaking > 0` and `lastSelfInjectionMessageId === lastMessageId()` (`effectsApplier.ts:474`). | as cited |
| E. `talk_control` / `chain` | Never runs without a player send (`talkControl.ts:213-221`). | |
| F. Per-member `guidance` ("In your first line, ask {{user}}…") | It lasts for the whole beat and reaches every one of that member's turns. There is no one-shot form. | `pacing/guidance.ts:18-28` |

The closest recipe that works today:
- No identity step.
- A `new_chat_only` scripted narrator opener that does **not** end on a question and quotes no "you".
- A **scripted** (not llm) `onEnter` party line.

That line is fixed text, the recipe is fragile, and nothing in the authoring tools says so.

### 1.3 Other gaps and bugs in play

1. **`llm` npc_replies ignore `instruction` on every trigger** (`afterSpeak`, `sceneBreak`, `onEnter`, life agenda, commitments). The guide's own example (`story-guide.md:327`, "Oren answers what the guide just said…") does nothing beyond picking Oren. Severity: high, because documented behaviour is missing.
2. **No question-yield rule in the chain.** `decideChainSpeaker` only checks addressees of the *player's* line (`talkControl.ts:321-337`), then asks the judge or director whether "the scene has said all it needs" (`director.ts:57`, `prompt.ts:28`). Suppose Mara asks the player a direct question at chain step 1: with no judge, a director call can still choose another member, who then answers for the player or buries the question. `addressesPlayer` already exists (`openerDeferral.ts:24`) but the chain never uses it.
3. **Member-to-member address is not routed.** In the chain, `addressed()` reads only the latest player line (`talkControl.ts:454-457`). A narrator line like "Mara, what do you make of this?" goes to Mara only if the judge's `addr:` question catches it. The judge needs a role on every candidate (`directorEligible.ts:9`), or it records `no-roles` and the rules pick. `directlyAddressed` (`directAddress.ts:41`) solves exactly this and only `engine/life/cues.ts:114` uses it.
4. **The lead bias crowds out the party.** After a player line `sceneWork` is true (`talkControl.ts:447-452`), so the rules return the lead first (`rules.ts:131-135`) and the judge adds `DIRECTOR_LEAD_BONUS` (`director.ts:96-98`). The narrator answers almost every player line. The next chain step offers the judge "PLAYER", and narrator prose usually reads as "said all it needs", so party members speak mostly when named. There is no memory across turns beyond `lastSpeaker` and `no_repeat` inside one chain (`rules.ts:128`), so nothing rotates the spotlight.
5. **`NO_CLOSING_QUESTION_CLAUSE` reaches every speaker.** It is in every pacing hint whenever `never_narrate_player_action` is on (the default) (`steering.ts:24-25`, `agency.ts:32`), and the pacing block is shared (`injectionRegistry.ts:30`). The rule targets narrator lines like "What do you do?", but a 31B model reads "never on a question asking {{user}}…" broadly, so it suppresses the in-character question the owner wants. *Suspected; measure it (section 3).*
6. **Beats without `talk_control` get no pacing control.** ST's native activation (talkativeness rolls) decides, with no chain, no hand-back and no yield (`runtimeManager.ts:381-384`).
7. ***Suspected race:* `afterSpeak` replies can collide with the chain.** `fireAfterSpeak` runs on every rendered reply (`turnBridge.ts:208`) while `TalkController.finishWrapper` starts the next voice (`talkControl.ts:246-265,302`). `fireReply` uses a raw `/trigger` or `/sendas` without `startVoice`'s busy and draft guards (`effectSteps.ts:69-71` vs `voiceStart.ts:16-17`). Two things could go wrong: a double voice, and an `afterSpeak` line posting after a character asked the player a question. `talkChainYield.review.test.ts` covers only the player's own send.
8. **`NPC_REPLY_SPACING = 8` messages is fixed** (`npcReplyRewind.ts:19`). With 3-voice chains that is about 2 player turns, and authors can't change it.
9. **`onEnter` never re-fires on a revisit**: the count is per checkpoint id and persists (`npcReplyRewind.ts:31`). There is no way to say "every time we come back here".
10. **`addressesPlayer` gives false positives**: any "you" in the last two quoted lines counts, even between two NPCs (`openerDeferral.ts:29`). It is fine as a deferral hint, too loose as a gate.
11. **Cost on one GPU.** The LLM director falls back to the memory model with a 20 s timeout per chain step (`talkControl.ts:18`, `wiring/talk.ts:33`). On the local 3090 it queues behind or between loud generations. The judge path costs 1.5 s and runs remotely (`policy.ts:43`). Any new per-step decision should use the judge request or deterministic rules, not another local LLM call.
12. **Nothing measures pacing.** `target_turn_length` only feeds the stall re-read (`extraction/reconcile.ts:78`). No metric covers speaker share, questions to the player, or monologue length.

## 2. Pacing controls: brainstorm

Shared constraints:
- Thinking stays on: never cap length through `max_tokens`, because the thinking tokens share that budget.
- No narrated player decisions.
- Rollback: anything persisted is keyed to message positions; everything else is derived from the chat.
- Chance: use `chance.talkRandom` and `npcRoll`, never `Math.random`.

### P1. One-shot cue: actually deliver `instruction` (fixes 1.3-1)
- **Schema:** none new. Optionally a `cue` enum on npc_replies (P3).
- **Mechanism:**
  - Before `fireReply`'s `/trigger`, arm a pending cue `{chatId, memberChid, afterMessageId, text}`.
  - The generate interceptor adds it as a new WITHHOLD injection `story_orchestrator_cue` at depth 0 to the private block of the drafted member. A good placement is beside `renderOwnAims` (`memory/innerRender.ts:85-92`) as "Your move this turn: …". It is added only when the forced chid matches.
  - The cue is cleared on render, stop, chat move or run lapse.
  - Nothing persists, so a swipe of that reply regenerates without the cue, unless the swipe path re-arms it from the fired record (decide which; I lean towards re-arming for swipes of an npc_reply line).
- **Cost:** 0 extra calls.
- **Risk:** the cue leaks into the prose. Mitigate by phrasing it like the inner-beat line and running the warden/leak check on it.
- **Test:** a jest seam test (cue present only for that chid and that generation). Live: an afterSpeak llm reply with the instruction "ask {{user}} about the map", run 5 times; the reply contains a question in ≥4.

### P2. `opening` sequence (the owner's ask)
- **Schema** (start checkpoint, or story-level):
  ```json
  "opening": {
    "beats": [
      { "member": "Ruins Narrator", "kind": "scripted", "text": "You came to Lowmarsh…" },
      { "member": "Mara", "kind": "llm", "cue": "address_player",
        "instruction": "Turn to {{user}} and ask what brought them to the marsh." }
    ],
    "yield": true
  }
  ```
- **Mechanism:**
  - One owned unit, checked for an empty chat **once** before the first post (this fixes route B).
  - It runs only after `requirements.ready && identitySettled` (fixes the race in route C).
  - `holdOpener` is skipped for its own steps, and the beats post in order through P1.
  - Its posts go into `onEnterPosts` so swipe and delete roll them back (`recordOnEnterPost`).
  - `yield: true` ends with a recorded `kind: "player"` talk decision so `yourMove` shows "your move" (`yourMove.ts:30-38`).
  - Lints: a `first_mes` on any card in the start cast (warn); an `llm` beat without `instruction` (error); an opening beat member not enabled at the start beat (error).
- **Cost:** +1 loud generation at chat start (the party line).
- **Risk:** the narrator's last line already asks the player, which gives two questions. A lint plus a guide line handle it ("let the narrator set the scene; the party member asks").
- **Test:** journey `J-open` on a fresh chat. Expect: message 0 from the narrator, message 1 from Mara containing `?` and the player's name or "you", and no third AI message within 10 s. Swipe message 1 → a new line arrives, still cued. Delete message 1 → it does not repost (a deletion is a choice).

### P3. Cue vocabulary
- **Schema:** `cue: "address_player" | "react" | "hand_to:<member>" | "aside"` on npc_replies, opening beats and chain steps.
- **Mechanism:** fixed templates in one pure module (`talk/cues.ts`), plus optional `instruction`.
  - `address_player`: "In character, speak to {{user}} directly and end on one question for them. Do not answer it or say what {{user}} does."
  - `react`: "One or two sentences reacting to what {{last_speaker}} just said."
  - `hand_to:X`: "End by turning to X."
  - All of them carry the agency clauses.
- **Cost:** 0. **Risk:** template drift. Golden-test the strings.
- **Test:** jest renders, plus the P2 journey.

### P4. Question-yield rule (fixes 1.3-2, partly 1.3-7)
- **Schema:** `talk_control.yield_on_question` (default `true`). It also applies, through one shared check, to beats with no `talk_control` once an opening or cue has fired.
- **Mechanism:**
  - Add a pure `asksPlayer(text, playerNames)`: the last *spoken* segment (`spokenSegments`, `directAddress.ts:12`) ends in `?`, and it either names the player in vocative position (reuse `vocativeIn`) or contains a second-person pronoun with no other roster member in vocative position. That is stricter than `addressesPlayer`.
  - In `advanceChain`, check it before `decideChainSpeaker`. On a hit, end the chain with a recorded `player` decision (source `rules`).
  - Suppress `afterSpeak` replies while the latest decision is `player` for this revision.
- **Cost:** 0 calls, and it **saves** judge or director calls.
- **Risk:** a rhetorical question stops the chain early. That is acceptable: the player may simply speak.
- **Test:** jest table of 30 recorded lines (positives and NPC-to-NPC negatives); a review test where the chain stops after the step-1 question and no afterSpeak line fires.

### P5. Spotlight rotation
- **Schema:** `talk_control.spotlight: { members?: string[], every: 2, cue: "react" | "address_player" }`. The default members are the non-lead speakers.
- **Mechanism:**
  - Derive from the chat window (no state): the player turns since each spotlight member last spoke.
  - After the lead answers a player line, if the most-neglected member is ≥ `every` player turns stale and the chain has room, the next chain step is that member, with the cue.
  - Ties go to the seeded `talkRandom`. P4 still wins, so a question to the player ends the chain first.
- **Cost:** +0 to 1 loud generation on some turns (it replaces a hand-back).
- **Risk:** forced, unmotivated interjections. Use the `react` cue by default, and let the judge veto with `reason:` < 0.2 when available.
- **Test:** jest over a synthetic window; metric: party share (section 3).

### P6. Lead monologue cap
- **Schema:** `talk_control.lead_length: "short" | "medium"`, or `{ paragraphs: 2 }`.
- **Mechanism:** a prompt cue only, in the lead's private block ("At most two short paragraphs; end on something that just happened"), plus a measurement. **Not** `max_tokens`, because thinking shares that budget and replies would be truncated.
- **Cost:** 0. **Risk:** the model ignores it. The metric decides whether it is worth keeping.
- **Test:** p90 word count of lead messages before and after (section 3).

### P7. Reaction beats
This is P1 + P3 applied to `afterSpeak`: `{trigger: "afterSpeak", member: "Oren", after_member: "Mara", kind: "llm", cue: "react", probability: 0.5}`.
- Make `spacing` authorable per reply (`spacing_turns`, counted in **player turns**, not messages, to fix 1.3-8).
- **Cost:** +1 generation when it fires.
- **Test:** an existing `npcReplyRoll` style test plus a spacing test.

### P8. Idle and stall nudges
- **Schema:** `pacing.nudge: { after_player_turns: 3, member?: string, cue: "aside" }`.
- **Mechanism:** turn-based, **not** wall-clock (a timer is not rollback-safe, and the owner may simply be reading or thinking).
  - Trigger: the quiet read holds (`pacing/quiet.ts` generalised beyond open stretches) and the player's last N lines are short or passive.
  - Effect: the next chain gets a spotlight step with an `aside` cue, in which a party member offers an observation or option. Options are never presented as a menu and are never acted on for the player.
  - An opt-in wall-clock nudge, if ever built, is a single cued line, once per "your move", and off by default.
- **Cost:** +1 generation rarely. **Risk:** it nags. Cap it at one per beat visit.

### P9. Per-checkpoint pacing profiles
- **Schema:** `pacing_profile: "conversation" | "exploration" | "combat" | "cutscene" | "confession"`.
- **Mechanism:** a pure expansion in `validate/checkpoints.ts` into `talk_control` defaults. The explicit fields win.

  | profile | chain max | lead_length | spotlight.every | yield | allow_silence |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | conversation | 3 | short | 1 | on | on |
  | exploration | 2 | medium | 2 | on | off |
  | combat | 4 | short | 1 | on | off |
  | cutscene | 4 | medium | off | off until the last step | off |
  | confession | 1 (`chain: false`) | short | off | on | off |

  A Studio picker, and the wizard sets it.
- **Cost:** 0. **Risk:** a profile hides behaviour from authors. Show the expanded values in Studio.
- **Test:** expansion jest; diagnostics.

### P10. Judge "is it the player's turn?"
- **Mechanism:** add `turn:player` as a noul question to the existing director request (`director.ts:54-58`): "Does the latest message ask {{player}} something or leave a choice waiting for them?" No extra call. Use it as a second signal behind P4, and offer hand-back at the *first* decision too, so a player line that is pure acting can go straight back.
- **Cost:** +1 question per judge call.
- **Risk:** this is a new judge use, so it needs a calibration row before it can route (`architecture.md` §Judge). Until then it stays off, with P4 as the floor.
- **Test:** a calibration golden of 40 labelled windows; floor ≥ 0.85 agreement.

### P11. Member-addressed routing in the chain (fixes 1.3-3)
- If `directlyAddressed(roster, lastVoiceText)` returns a fresh candidate, it is next (source `mention`), before the judge.
- **Cost:** 0, and it saves calls.
- **Test:** jest plus a review test.

### P12. Scope the closing-question clause by speaker (fixes 1.3-5)
- Split `NO_CLOSING_QUESTION_CLAUSE`. The lead gets "never end on 'what do you do?'"; members get "you may ask {{user}} an in-character question; never answer it for them".
- It moves from the shared pacing block to each speaker's private block.
- **Cost:** 0. **Risk:** prompt layout is quality-rated (`architecture.md` §Local text backend), so this needs a blind rating and a cache check before adoption.
- **Test:** metrics plus blind ratings (section 3).

### P13. Beat-entry hooks for transitions
- Use P2's `beats` as `effects.on_enter` (`{beats, yield}`) for any checkpoint: narration of the new beat, then a party reaction or question.
- It reuses the opening unit, with `holdOpener` kept for the transition case, where it is right.

### P14. Authoring lints and guide
- `llm-reply-without-instruction`
- `opener-asks-player-before-onEnter` (the party line will be deferred)
- `start-cast-first-mes`
- `party-silent-beat` (info: a group beat where no non-lead can be picked without being named)
- Update the guide topics `npc-replies`, `opening-scene` and `talk-control`, then regenerate them with `npm run docs:guide`.

## 3. Evaluation design

### 3.1 Deterministic metrics
A new `so-talk-metrics.mts` reads the journal or chat export. It reuses `asksPlayer`, `directlyAddressed` and the roster. A *round* is every AI message between two player messages.

Floors are predeclared and informational, per AGENTS.md: record a miss, never retune afterwards.

| Metric | Definition | Floor |
|---|---|---|
| Opening hook rate | Fresh chats in which a non-lead member addresses the player before the player's first message | ≥ 0.95 where an `opening` is authored |
| Turns-to-first-address (TFA) | AI messages before the first non-lead line that `asksPlayer` | ≤ 2 in ≥ 0.9 of openings |
| Question-yield integrity | After a line that `asksPlayer`, the next message is the player's | ≥ 0.97 |
| Answered-for-player rate | AI lines after a question to the player that answer it or narrate the player's act (rater label) | ≤ 0.02 |
| Player-question rate | Rounds whose last AI line asks the player something | in [0.3, 0.6]; above 0.6 reads as an interrogation |
| Narrator "what do you do" rate | Lead lines ending on a generic action prompt | ≤ 0.1 |
| Party share | Non-lead AI messages ÷ all AI messages, per session | ≥ 0.3 |
| Spotlight gap | Longest run of consecutive rounds with no non-lead voice | ≤ 3 |
| Lead monologue | p50 / p90 words per lead message | p90 ≤ 220 (owner to confirm) |
| Round size | AI messages per player send | mean in [1.3, 2.2]; max ≤ chain max |
| Round latency | Wall time per round on the local 31B | p50 ≤ +15 % vs baseline |
| Decision overhead | Judge or director calls per round | not above baseline (P4 and P11 should lower it) |

### 3.2 Blind transcript ratings
- **Arms:** baseline (master) vs candidate. Same stories, same seeds, same opening.
- **Player lines:**
  - Player lines must adapt to the transcript, so use an LLM player (opencode `gpt-6.1-sol`, never Artemis) with a fixed persona card.
  - The first 2 player lines are scripted and identical across arms.
- **Volume:** 12 fresh openings × 2 stories × 2 arms, plus 6 sessions × 15 player turns per arm. Run twice (the twice-on-one-lane rule).
- **Rating pairs:** order randomised, arm labels stripped.
- **Rubric (1–5):**
  1. I was pulled into the scene quickly.
  2. It felt like a party, not one narrator.
  3. I had room to act.
  4. Nobody spoke or decided for me.
  5. The pace felt neither rushed nor dragging.
- **Floors:**
  - The candidate wins ≥ 60 % of pairs on items 1 and 2.
  - Item 4 is non-inferior (mean drop ≤ 0.2).
  - Item 3 is non-inferior.
- **Raters:**
  - The delegated rater model rates everything.
  - The owner rates only non-campaign test stories (public fixtures), never Adolion transcripts (no spoilers).
  - Adolion is rated by the model only.
- **Evidence:** archive in `so-sessions`.

### 3.3 Gates per change
- **Pure (P3, P4, P9, P11, the asksPlayer table):** `npm run gates:quick`, then `npm run gates`.
- **P1, P2, P5, P12** touch generation, so they need the real-LLM live gate on a lane: the `J-open` journey ×2, plus one metrics session.
- **P12** also needs the payload-golden diff and the blind rating, because it moves prompt text.

## Unresolved questions

1. When the player swipes a cued npc_reply line, should the cue be re-armed? (I lean yes.)
2. Should deleting a cued opening line repost it, or treat the deletion as a choice? (I lean towards treating it as a choice.)
3. Lead monologue floor: is about 220 words at p90 right for the owner's taste?
4. Spotlight default: `react` or `address_player`? Too many questions turn play into an interrogation.
5. Should the opening live on the start checkpoint (P13 reuse) or at story level?
6. Is an opt-in wall-clock idle nudge wanted at all?
