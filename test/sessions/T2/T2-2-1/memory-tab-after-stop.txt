Max left the study to find the household gathered uneasily in the hall, with Shiya watching from the edge and Welden refusing to meet anyone's gaze. Lord Javon emerged and decreed that Natalia would serve as the Crown's second in the Trial by Combat in three days, and Natalia responded with cold defiance, accusing him of sending her to her grave and refusing to beg. Later, Max found Natalia by the stairs and warned her that the old well behind the stables was poisoned and that Father was hiding it, which she agreed to keep secret while noting it was likely worse than the duel. Max then questioned Shiya in the servants' corridor about the well, and she confirmed she had heard nothing but reaffirmed that the seals were failing and that something was deeply wrong in the house. Finally, Max approached Ronan at his post, and Ronan confirmed that Shiya had told him the seals were weakening and that Max wanted Natalia kept away from it, adding that Shiya had asked whether Max had any plans.
