## Integration follow-up (2026-09-25): a single-pipe reason after the curator's target
Answers like "[enable] Title | reason" are off the prompted format, but a real model may add them, and T17.2's exact matching would drop them. `readOp` now retries once with the text before " | " when the whole target matches nothing.
